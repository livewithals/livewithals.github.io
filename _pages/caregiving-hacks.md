Good care for a PALS rarely follows a textbook. Over the years, we've learned that
resourcefulness and creativity are just as important as medical knowledge. This page
is where we share the tips, tricks, and homegrown solutions that have made a real
difference for us.

## 1. Innovative nebulizer solution for keeping the nebulizer cup upright

During nebulization, keeping the nebulizer cup perfectly vertical is critical for
effective drug delivery to the lungs. We solved this with **3 "T Pieces"** arranged
in a specific configuration. Unnecessary holes can be blocked using stoppers — we
use the cap of "Fast & Up Reload" instant electrolytes for this purpose.

## 2. Using an Ambu bag as a manual ventilator

An Ambu bag is a valuable dual-purpose tool:

- **Routine use** — lung volume recruitment, squeezes at 12–16 per minute matching
  the patient's natural breathing pace
- **Emergency use** — when the primary ventilator fails; connect supplemental oxygen
  and increase rate until saturation stabilizes

<div class="notice--als-tip">
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> Always keep a charged Ambu bag within arm's reach of the patient at all times.</p>
</div>

---

## 3. Simultaneous nebulization and Ambu bagging

Combining nebulization with Ambu ventilation simultaneously serves two purposes:

1. Drives nebulized medication **further into the lungs** for better absorption
2. Pressure from Ambu compressions **pushes secretions upward** into the trachea,
   making suctioning significantly easier and more effective

## 4. Make your enema solution at home

We have found the **"Neotomic" enema** (intended for kids) to work best for our
PALS with no side effects since February 2021. However, commercially available
Neotomic in India costs approximately ₹70 for 20 ml (as of Feb 2024), making
home synthesis practical.

### Composition of Neotomic enema

| Component | Concentration |
|-----------|--------------|
| Sodium Chloride (NaCl) | 15% w/v (15 g per 100 ml) |
| Glycerine (density 1.26 g/ml) | 15% w/v (15 g per 100 ml) |
| Water (density 1.00 g/ml) | Balance |

### Our synthesis recipe

Pour into a **sterile bottle** and mix well:

- **587 ml** normal saline
- **95 g** sterile, non-iodized salt
- **100 g** glycerine

This yields approximately **680 ml**, sufficient for ~17 uses at 40 ml per enema
(from experience, 30 ml is often sufficient). Store at room temperature in a sterile
airtight container.

<div class="notice--als-warning">
<p><strong>Important:</strong> All components must be acquired and processed in the most sterile manner possible. Consult your physician before making any changes to the patient's care protocol.</p>
</div>
