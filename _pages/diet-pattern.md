The PEG feeding pattern we follow for our father can be summarised as follows:

## Three Major Solid Meals

1. **Breakfast** — 300 ml + 100 ml flush &nbsp;|&nbsp; 09:30 AM
2. **Lunch** — 450 ml + 100 ml flush &nbsp;|&nbsp; 03:30 PM
3. **Dinner** — 450 ml + 100 ml flush &nbsp;|&nbsp; 09:30 PM

Major solid meals reflect his personal likings, eating habits from the past, and current health conditions. We give him a usual Indian diet: Oats, Dal, Daliya, Roti, Sabji, Daal, Chawal, Dosa, Rajma, Kabuli Chana, Paneer, etc. No spices except raai, jeera, haldi and salt. Acidic/sour feeds are avoided.

**Running total: 1200 ml solid, 300 ml liquid.**

## Six Fillers / Fluids

Nutritious fluids are used as fillers throughout the day. The choice varies with season — juices, buttermilk, coconut water, plain water (with electrolyte if necessary), milk (before bedtime).

Usually 6 fillers daily: 4 fillers of 250 ml and 2 fillers of 300 ml. Supplements and most medicines are given with these fillers.

**Running total: 1200 ml solid, 1900 ml liquid.**

## Early Morning Plain Water

1. 200 ml plain water for Thyroid medication
2. After 90 minutes — 200 ml plain water for Pantaprozole

**Grand total: 1200 ml solid, 2300 ml liquid.**

<div class="notice--als-tip">
<p><strong>Important:</strong> We keep at least 90 minutes of gap between two fillers, as well as between a filler and a solid meal.</p>
</div>
